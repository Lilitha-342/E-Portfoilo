## Portfolio overview

This portfolio consolidates the saved E-Khaya Connect logbooks, presentation and Khayelitsha WISP solution artefacts. Where formal agreements or assignments were not explicitly recorded, the content is marked as a proposed working version for the group to confirm.


------
# Sprint review and retrospective

What went well: regular meetings, collaborative prototype work, brand-guide completion and review of documentation. Challenges included limited time, scheduling, colour feedback and formatting. Improvement actions: keep the taskboard current, agree review deadlines earlier, record decisions immediately and reserve time for final quality checks.

-----
# Reflection and Lessons Learned

The logbooks show regular collaboration, prototype development, brand-guide completion and team review meetings. These activities helped the group organise work into manageable tasks. Time pressure, member availability and minor design feedback highlighted the importance of visible task ownership, early review and a protected final quality-assurance period.

For the next iteration, the team should test the prototype with potential customers, validate the R399 price and technical coverage assumptions, and connect the contact form to a secure real service before collecting personal information.
